Sidi Wu

I'm a postdoc researcher at ETH Zurich, affliated with the Institute of Cartography and Geoinformation. I'm also an associate partner of ETH AI Center and ETH Agentic Systems Lab.

I obtained my PhD degree from ETH Zurich, supervised by Prof. Lorenz Hurni and Prof. Konrad Schindler. During my PhD, I had a research visit at LASTIG Lab, IGN, the French national mapping agency, where I worked closely with Loic Landrieu, Julien Perret, Clément Mallet and Nicolas Gonthier. Prior to that, I obtained my master's degree in Geodesy and Geoinformation from TU Munich and my bachelor's degree (Honoured Graduate) in Geoinformation from Wuhan University.

I'm a geospatial AI researcher focusing on integrating heterogeneous (historic) geospatial data with deep learning to understand long-term changes in urban and enviromental systems. I'm also intersted in human-centered AI that lowers barriers of map-making and geospatial applications to non-experts. Beyond geospatial research, I am interested in transferring spatial AI methods to other scientific domains, including design and health.
